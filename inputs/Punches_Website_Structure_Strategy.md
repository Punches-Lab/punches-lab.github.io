## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This document outlines a professional, research-focused website architecture for Dr. Brittany Punches and her leadership role in **HealthNow** (a Population Health & Health Services program at Ohio State University Emergency Medicine). The site should position her as a health services researcher; program leader; mentor; and expert in pain, addiction, trauma recovery, and health equity. It will serve dual purposes: institutional visibility and academic credibility.

**Key Strategic Goals:**
- Establish Dr. Punches as a visible, accessible researcher and leader
- Promote HealthNow clinical services and impact to diverse audiences
- Showcase research rigor, student mentorship, and translational work
- Support grant visibility and recruitment efforts
- Bridge academic, clinical, and community audiences

**Current Projects to Feature:**

**1. Decision-Making Factors for Therapeutic Opioid Use after Emergency Care**
- **Funding:** NIH/NIDA K08DA049948 (PI: B. Punches)
- **Award Period:** April 2021–March 2026
- **Co-Investigators:** Michael S. Lyons, MD, MPH (mentor role)
- **Objective:** Develop and psychometrically test a decision-making instrument to understand and predict opioid use patterns after ED discharge for acute pain, informing interventions to prevent opioid use disorder.
- **Methods:** Mixed-methods prospective cohort study; 3-month follow-up assessments; ED sites across Midwest
- **Impact:** Instrument will enable ED clinicians and providers to identify patients at risk for problematic opioid use and deliver targeted interventions
- **Key Publications:** 
  - Punches BE, et al. (2022). "Predicting at-risk opioid use three months after an emergency department visit for acute pain." *PLOS ONE* [Link]
  - Punches BE, et al. (2024). "Development and Validation of the Decisions to use Opioids Measure." *Pain Management Nursing* [Link]
  - Punches BE, et al. (2024). "Patient motivators to use opioids for acute pain after emergency care." *Frontiers in Pain Research* [Link]

**2. Screening for Problematic Opioid Use in the Emergency Department**
- **Status:** Completed peer review; manuscript published
- **Objective:** Compare two validated screening measures (DSM-5 Checklist vs. WHO ASSIST 3.1) for opioid use disorder in ED patients; determine prevalence of OUD and substance use patterns
- **Methods:** Cross-sectional study; 3 Midwestern ED sites; 1,000+ adult patients
- **Impact:** Informs ED screening protocols and identifies disparities (by sex, race, employment status) in OUD assessment via item response theory analysis
- **Publication:** Punches BE, Freiermuth CE, et al. (2024). "Screening for problematic opioid use in the emergency department: Comparison of two screening measures." *JACEP Open*, 5(1), e13106. [Link]

**3. Genetic Markers Associated with Opioid Use Disorder**
- **Status:** Completed
- **Role:** Co-Investigator
- **PI:** Caroline E. Freiermuth (University of Cincinnati)
- **Objective:** Identify genetic variants associated with OUD among Ohio residents
- **Publication:** Freiermuth CE, et al. (2023). "Genetic Variants Associated With Opioid Use Disorder." *Clinical Pharmacology and Therapeutics* [Link]

**4. Naloxone Distribution Program Evaluation: Per Capita Distribution and Overdose Mortality**
- **Status:** Completed
- **Funding:** Ohio Department of Health; CDC partnership
- **Objective:** Evaluate statewide naloxone distribution program impact on overdose mortality in Ohio, with attention to geographic and population disparities
- **Role:** Co-Investigator
- **Publication:** Freiermuth CE, et al. (2023). "Evaluation of a large-scale health department naloxone distribution program: Per capita naloxone distribution and overdose mortality." *PLOS ONE* [Link]

**5. Longitudinal Assessment of Post-Traumatic Syndromes (AURORA Study)**
- **Status:** Active
- **Funding:** NIH/NIMH U01MH110925 (PI: Samuel A. McLean, UNC)
- **Role:** Site Principal Investigator
- **OSU Sites:** Multiple EDs across Ohio system
- **Objective:** Prospective, longitudinal cohort study examining mechanisms and predictors of adverse post-traumatic outcomes (depression, anxiety, PTSD); explore role of sociodemographic, psychosocial, and biological factors
- **Methods:** 1,305+ trauma-exposed ED patients; baseline and 3-month assessments; validated screening instruments (PROMIS, Primary Care PTSD Screen)
- **OSU Sub-Studies:** 
  - Brain-based biotypes of psychiatric vulnerability (Stevens et al., *AJP*, 2021)
  - Classification of post-trauma outcomes using wrist-worn sensors and circadian rhythm data (Cakmak et al., *IEEE JBHI*, 2021)
  - Multi-level socioeconomic modifiers of comorbidity between PTSD and substance use (Meyers et al., *Neurobiology of Stress*, in prep)
- **Publications:** 10+ papers co-authored by Punches; ongoing analysis

**6. Patient Perceptions of Microaggressions and Discrimination in Emergency Care**
- **Status:** Completed; published
- **Objective:** Qualitative study exploring patient experiences of discrimination, microaggressions, and unfair treatment in the ED; link to health outcomes and care-seeking behavior
- **Methods:** Prospective interview study; diverse sample of ED patients; qualitative thematic analysis
- **Impact:** Highlights need for cultural competency training and systemic anti-racism work in ED settings
- **Publication:** Southerland LT, Punches BE, et al. (2023). "Patient perceptions of microaggressions and discrimination toward patients during emergency department care." *Academic Emergency Medicine* [Link]

**7. Emergency Nurses' Perspectives on Pain, Addiction, and Opioid Overdose**
- **Status:** Completed
- **Funding:** Emergency Nurses Association Foundation
- **Objective:** Understand nurse perspectives on pain management, opioid prescribing, and overdose risk; inform educational interventions
- **Methods:** Survey of emergency nurses; descriptive analysis
- **Publications:**
  - Punches BE, et al. (2021). "Emergency Nurse Perceptions of Pain and Opioids in the Emergency Department." *Pain Management Nursing*, 22(5), 586–591. [Link]
  - Punches BE, et al. (2021). "Opioid-Related Risk Screening Measures for the Emergency Care Setting: A Systematic Review." *Advanced Emergency Nursing Journal* [Link]

**8. ED-Based HIV Screening & Linkage to Care (HIV TESTED Trial)**
- **Status:** Active/Completed
- **Role:** Co-Investigator
- **PI:** Jason Haukoos, MD, MPH (multiple sites)
- **Objective:** Randomized clinical trial comparing HIV screening strategies in the ED; cost-effectiveness analysis
- **OSU Contribution:** Site enrollment and data collection
- **Publication:** Haukoos J, Lyons M, Punches B, et al. (2021). "Comparison of HIV Screening Strategies in the Emergency Department: A Randomized Clinical Trial." *JAMA Network Open* [Link]

**9. Health Equity & Pain Management Research Program (Disparities Focus)**
