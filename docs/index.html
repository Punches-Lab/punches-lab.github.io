About

Dr. Brittany Punches

Associate Professor in the College of Nursing and Department of Emergency Medicine at The Ohio State University. Dr. Punches leads a health services research program focused on advancing equitable, evidence-based emergency care for patients with pain, substance use, and trauma-related conditions.

PhD, MPH, RN, CEN, FAEN — Expert in emergency care, opioid decision-making, substance use disorder screening, and health equity. Over 1,000 citations across 108 peer-reviewed publications.

Clinical Program

HealthNow: ED-Based Health Promotion & Prevention

HealthNow provides screening, counseling, and linkage-to-care services in the emergency department to address HIV, Hepatitis C, substance use disorder, and other prevalent public health issues.

Part of the Population Health & Health Services (PH2S) program at OSU Emergency Medicine, HealthNow serves over 130,000 ED patients annually across Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center.
