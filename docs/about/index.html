Biography

Brittany Punches, PhD, MPH, RN, CEN, FAEN

Dr. Brittany Punches is an Associate Professor in the College of Nursing and Department of Emergency Medicine at The Ohio State University, where she leads a health services research program focused on advancing equitable, evidence-based emergency care interventions.

Dr. Punches brings a unique perspective to her research, having trained first as a clinical emergency nurse (RN, CEN) before pursuing graduate training in nursing science and public health. This clinical foundation shapes her commitment to translational research—work that not only advances scientific understanding but directly informs practice, policy, and the care that patients receive in emergency departments.

Her research program uses mixed-methods approaches—combining quantitative surveys, psychometric instrument development, and qualitative inquiry—to examine how patients navigate pain management and substance use decision-making after emergency care. A central and explicit priority across all of her work is health equity: understanding and dismantling the disparities that shape how patients with pain and substance use disorders are assessed and treated in emergency settings.

As Assistant Director of HealthNow and a faculty leader in OSU's Population Health and Health Services (PH2S) program, Dr. Punches bridges the worlds of clinical care and academic research—translating findings into real programs that serve vulnerable, high-risk populations in the emergency department.

Dr. Punches is deeply committed to mentorship. She views training the next generation of nurse scientists and health services researchers as inseparable from the research mission itself—and has mentored five or more graduate students over her career, with an approach that emphasizes individualized development, research rigor, and an explicit commitment to equity and inclusion.

Mission & Vision

Research Program Values

Our Mission

"To understand and improve emergency care experiences for patients with pain, substance use, and trauma-related conditions, with an explicit focus on reducing health disparities and preventing harm."

Our Vision

"Emergency departments as frontline settings for evidence-based health promotion, early intervention, and equitable care that honors patient autonomy and dignity."

Core Values

Patient-Centered Research

All work begins with understanding the patient experience. Research questions are grounded in what matters to patients navigating emergency care.

Health Equity

Explicit attention to disparities in pain management, SUD assessment, and trauma care across race, ethnicity, gender, employment, and socioeconomic status.

Evidence-Based Translation

Rigorous research that informs policy and practice change—science that closes the gap between what we know and how care is delivered.

Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Partnerships across nursing science, emergency medicine, psychology, pharmacy, public health, and community organizations.

Mentorship & Development

Commitment to training the next generation of clinician-scientists and nurse researchers through individualized, equity-centered mentorship.

Transparency & Rigor

Mixed-methods approaches, open science, and honest acknowledgment of limitations in study design and interpretation.
