Recent Publications

2024
Research · K08 Project

Development and Validation of the Decisions to Use Opioids Measure

Dr. Punches' K08-funded study publishes the Decisions to Use Opioids Measure (DOM)—a validated instrument for understanding patient opioid decision-making after emergency department discharge. Pain Management Nursing.

2024
Research · Health Equity

Screening for Problematic Opioid Use in the ED: Comparison of Two Measures

New cross-sectional study at 3 Midwestern EDs compares DSM-5 Checklist vs. WHO ASSIST 3.1 for OUD screening and identifies disparities by sex, race, and employment status. JACEP Open, 5(1), e13106.

2023
Research · Health Equity

Patient Perceptions of Microaggressions and Discrimination in Emergency Care

Qualitative study examining patient experiences of discrimination in the ED highlights need for cultural competency training and anti-racism initiatives. Academic Emergency Medicine.

2023
Research · Naloxone

Statewide Naloxone Distribution Program Evaluation Published

Evaluation of Ohio Department of Health naloxone distribution program finds population-level impact on overdose mortality. PLOS ONE.

2022
Research · K08 Project

Predicting At-Risk Opioid Use After Emergency Care

New prospective cohort study identifies predictors of at-risk opioid use at 3 months post-ED discharge for acute pain. PLOS ONE.
