About the Program

HealthNow: Population Health & Prevention in the ED

HealthNow provides screening, counseling, and linkage-to-care services in the emergency department to address HIV, Hepatitis C, substance use disorder, and other prevalent public health issues—with special attention to patients who are underserved or high-risk.

A program of the Population Health & Health Services (PH2S) unit within the Department of Emergency Medicine at Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center, HealthNow serves more than 130,000 ED patients annually across OSU's East and Main Campus emergency departments.

HealthNow interventions are informed by peer-reviewed implementation science in ED-based screening effectiveness, nurse-driven prevention models, trauma-informed care, and culturally competent approaches to reduce health disparities.

Key Services

Screening

Universal Screening

Brief, evidence-based screening for HIV, Hepatitis C, substance use disorder, and other public health priorities—offered to all ED patients.

Counseling

Assessment & Counseling

Motivational interviewing, harm reduction, and patient-centered goal-setting—meeting patients where they are without judgment.

Navigation

Linkage to Care

Connection to primary care, addiction treatment, infectious disease services, and community-based organizations—removing barriers to follow-through.

Support

Peer Support & Navigation

Help with transportation, insurance, appointment scheduling, and other practical barriers that prevent patients from accessing needed care.

Follow-Up

Post-Discharge Follow-Up

Our team checks in with patients after ED discharge to ensure care connections were successful and provide ongoing support.

For Patients

What Patients Can Expect

HealthNow is free, confidential, and available to any patient who visits the emergency department—regardless of insurance or primary care status.

What is HealthNow?

HealthNow is a program in the Emergency Department at Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center that helps patients with health concerns like HIV, Hepatitis C, and substance use issues. Our team is here to support you, answer questions, and connect you to care you need.

Who can use HealthNow?

All patients who visit the ED. You don’t need insurance or a primary care doctor. Our services are free and confidential.

What can I expect?

When you visit the ED, our Health Promotion Advocate will offer to screen you. You can say yes or no. If yes, we’ll spend private time with you to understand your needs—then discuss next steps, answer questions, and connect you with appropriate services.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Your information is private and protected. We cannot share it without your permission, except in rare safety situations required by law.

What if I don’t have insurance?

HealthNow works with you to find resources, apply for coverage, or connect you to services that help without insurance. No one is turned away.

What if I’m not ready for treatment?

That’s okay. We meet you where you are. Our goal is to provide information, remove barriers, and support you whenever you’re ready—without pressure or judgment.
