Mentorship

Approach to Mentorship

Dr. Punches views mentoring as central to her academic mission—inseparable from the research enterprise itself. She has mentored five or more graduate students and postdoctoral researchers over her career, with an approach grounded in individualized development, scientific rigor, and an explicit commitment to equity and inclusion.

Mentees have pursued careers in nursing academia, health services research, and clinical practice—many continuing work in the areas of emergency care, pain management, and health equity.

Mentorship Principles

Individualized Development Plans

Each mentee develops a customized plan aligned with their career goals—academic, clinical, or policy-focused.

Explicit Skill Building

Training in manuscript writing, presentations, grant writing, research design, qualitative analysis, and professional development.

Research Rigor & Impact

Focus on high-quality, translational work that closes the gap between science and clinical practice or policy.

Mentorship Teams

Encouragement to build relationships with multiple mentors across disciplines and institutions for broad professional development.

Work-Life Balance

Genuine recognition of the importance of wellness, family, and personal priorities in a sustainable research career.

Equity & Inclusion

Active commitment to supporting trainees from underrepresented backgrounds and creating an inclusive, anti-racist lab environment.

Graduate & Research Opportunities

Join the Lab

Graduate Research Positions

Dr. Punches regularly recruits motivated graduate students (MS, PhD) interested in health services research, nursing science, or interdisciplinary population health.

Strong candidates typically have:

  • Clinical background or strong health sciences foundation
  • Curiosity about emergency care, pain, substance use, or trauma
  • Commitment to health equity and addressing health disparities
  • Strong communication and writing skills
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced, team-based environment

Areas of Opportunity

  • K08 Opioid Decision-Making Project

    Continuing analysis and translation of the K08-funded Decisions to Use Opioids Measure (DOM) study.

  • AURORA Study Analysis

    Sub-studies on trauma, PTSD, and substance use comorbidity; wearable sensor data.

  • HealthNow Program Evaluation

    Implementation research examining HealthNow's reach, effectiveness, and equity.

  • Original Thesis/Dissertation Work

    Projects in the student's area of interest—within Dr. Punches' scope of emergency care, pain, SUD, or health equity.

Affiliated Graduate Programs

  • OSU College of Nursing — PhD in Nursing Science
  • OSU College of Public Health — PhD in Health Services Research; MPH
  • OSU Department of Emergency Medicine — Fellowship Programs
  • Ohio State Graduate School — Interdisciplinary options

How to Apply

If interested, please contact Dr. Punches at punches.5@osu.edu with the subject line "Graduate Research Position Inquiry" and include:

  • CV or résumé
  • Brief statement of research interests (1–2 paragraphs)
  • Names and contact information for 2–3 references

Positions are funded through research grants when available; information on graduate assistantships and fellowships is available upon inquiry.
